# Caption

Remember the first time you rode a bicycle? Or the first time you spoke a language that wasn't your mother tongue? Or your first hour on a computer at school?

New feels exciting and scary at once. Exciting because it's new. Scary because of everything we don't know yet. But not knowing something doesn't mean we can't learn it, and the scary part usually shrinks once we look at the details. So let's look.

Take AI. The news is loud: it's amazing, it's exciting, it's frightening, sometimes in the same headline.

I'm learning AI in public, so I started this blog. It's called AI Business Lab.

I work in finance and strategy, so that's where I start, not where I stop. I'll test AI on business problems wherever they show up, from a budget table to building my own team of agents.

I like things structured and easy to follow, and I'll break down the complex terms and ideas the way I wish someone had done for me.

Regularly, I'll run one real task through AI and show you what happened, mistakes included.

If you're starting too, come along. Let's share, help each other and learn together.

Cheers!
